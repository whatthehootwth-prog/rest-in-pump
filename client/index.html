What is Rest In Pump?

What is Rest In Pump?

It's a graveyard for memecoins that went to zero, and you can walk around it with other people. You play a confused little owl. Every other owl you see is someone online right now.

Why we made it

New memecoins launch every day. When the hype dies, the community drifts off, the jokes get forgotten, and nobody has a reason to come back.

Rest In Pump gives those coins a place that stays. Holders can visit, leave something silly at the grave, and laugh about how it all went.

What you can do

  • Walk around with other degens. Every owl gets its own strange walk.
  • Mourn in style: dance, club, pray, cry your eyes out, or roll on the ground.
  • Make a grave for a dead NEAR or Solana memecoin and leave gifts at any grave. Gifts give the grave EXP and level it up.
  • Get a Memorial NFT the first time you leave a gift at a grave.
  • Chat with everyone and take snapshots of the funeral.

Graves

Walk up to an empty plot and press E. Pick NEAR or Solana and paste the coin's token address (on Solana that is the mint address, the CA). We pull its name and logo from that chain. You still pay in NEAR either way. Then write its last words, up to 100 characters.

  • A grave costs $1, paid in NEAR at the current price. We check the payment on chain.
  • Each coin gets one grave, and graves stay forever.
  • Nobody owns a grave. It belongs to the graveyard and anyone can visit.
  • Making a grave doesn't give you an NFT. You get one by leaving a gift, same as everyone.

Gifts, EXP & levels

At any grave, press E and pick a gift. Each one has its own price (paid in NEAR) and gives the grave EXP. Bigger gifts give a little more EXP per dollar. Every gift keeps the memory alive.

Memorial NFTs

The first time your wallet leaves a gift at a grave, you get that grave's Memorial NFT on NEAR. It's named after the coin, like "$RUG Memorial #001".

Shown with a sample $RIP memorial. Yours carries the coin's own logo.

  • The art grows with the grave. Your memorial starts as the grave is and upgrades by itself as the grave gains EXP. A Forgotten one can end up Legendary.
  • One per wallet per grave. Leave ten gifts and you still have one memorial for that grave.
  • It records the coin, its contract, the grave, the grave's EXP, the date of your first gift and how many gifts you've left.
  • It's a keepsake. No staking, no yield, no rewards.

How Rest In Pump helps $WTH

Graves and offerings are paid in NEAR. The money collects in the game's buyback wallet. Each time it reaches $20, a buyback round runs by itself:

  • 20% goes to the WTH wallet, whatthehoot.near.
  • 80% buys $WTH on Ref Finance. The buy is skipped if the price would move more than 2%.
  • The $WTH it buys goes straight to holders, split by how much each one holds. You need at least 100,000 WTH. Pools, exchanges and the project's own wallets don't get a share.
  • Every round is public. You can see the amounts, who got what and every transaction below.
