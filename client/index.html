Welcome to the graveyard of fallen memecoins. Explore with other degens, visit the graves, and leave ridiculous offerings to keep their memory alive.

What is Rest In Pump?

What is Rest In Pump?

A multiplayer graveyard for memecoins that went to zero. You are a confused little owl, and every other owl you meet is a real person who is online right now.

Why it exists

Thousands of memecoins are created, but when their hype fades, their communities disappear, their stories are forgotten, and there's little reason for people to interact with them again.

Rest In Pump addresses this by giving memecoins a permanent place where communities can remember, revisit, and even celebrate their failures.

What you can do today

  • Wander the graveyard with other degens. Every owl is born with its own ridiculous walk.
  • Mourn properly: dance, club, pray, cry your heart out, or roll around on the ground.
  • Create a grave for a fallen NEAR memecoin, and leave offerings at any grave.
  • Earn a Memorial NFT with your first offering to a grave.
  • Chat with everyone and take snapshots of the funeral.

Graves

Walk up to an empty plot and press E to create a grave. Paste the meme's NEAR token contract — its name and logo are read straight from the chain — and leave its last words (up to 100 characters).

  • Creating a grave costs one fixed price in $WTH, verified on NEAR.
  • One grave per meme, forever. Graves can't be removed.
  • Nobody owns a grave — it belongs to the graveyard and everyone can visit it.
  • Creating a grave doesn't mint an NFT. Creators earn a memorial with an offering, like everyone else.

Offerings & levels

At any grave, press E to leave an offering: a candle, flowers, instant noodles, a rug, diamond hands or a copium tank — plus any amount of $WTH from 10,000 up. Every offering keeps the memory alive.

Memorial NFTs

The first time your wallet makes a successful offering to a grave, you receive that grave's Memorial NFT on NEAR — like “$RUG — Memorial #001”.

  • One per wallet per grave, however many offerings you make.
  • Every memorial of a grave evolves with it: the artwork and details always show its current level and offering count.
  • Its details record the meme, its contract, the grave, your first offering date and how many offerings you made.
  • It's a keepsake: no staking, no yield, no rewards.

How Rest In Pump helps $WTH

Everything you pay for in Rest In Pump is paid in $WTH, the What The Hoot token.

  • Demand: creating graves and leaving offerings needs $WTH, so every player is a $WTH holder.
  • Buyback: revenue from graves and offerings is used to buy back $WTH.
  • Transparent: payments go to the treasury wallet whatthehoot.near, which anyone can check on NearBlocks.

$WTH is a memecoin and can lose all its value.
