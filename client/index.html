Rest In Pump

Welcome to the graveyard of fallen memecoins. Explore with other degens, bury your dead bags, and leave ridiculous offerings to honor your losses.

What is Rest In Pump?

A multiplayer graveyard for memecoins that went to zero. You are a confused little owl, and every other owl you meet is a real person who is online right now.

What you can do today

  • Wander the graveyard with other degens. Every owl is born with its own ridiculous walk.
  • Mourn properly: dance, club, pray (拜拜), kneel and cry, or roll around on the ground.
  • Chat with everyone in the graveyard and see it pop up above their heads.
  • Take a snapshot of the funeral and share it.

Coming soon not live yet

  • Bury a meme: give your dead bag a grave of its own that stays in the graveyard for everyone to find.
  • Funerals and offerings: leave ridiculous offerings at a grave, paid in $WTH and verified on NEAR.
  • Grave search: find any buried coin and visit it.
  • Resurrections: if a buried coin pumps back from the dead, its grave will know.

None of this is switched on yet, and nothing in the game asks for money today.

Controls

Arrow keys or WASD to walk · Shift to run · Space to hop · 1–5 for emotes · P for a snapshot · Enter to chat · drag to turn the camera

Not financial advice. Memecoins can go to zero — that's why this place exists.
